Refund & Rescheduling Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Plain-language guidelines for demo sessions, batch enrollment, and transfers.

Zero-Risk Demo Session Commitment

We encourage all prospective students to attend our Free Weekend Demo Class before enrolling. This allows you to experience the instructor's live teaching methodology, curriculum depth, and interactive format firsthand with zero financial commitment.

1 Live Cohort Refund Window

If an enrolled student must withdraw from a live cohort:

  • Prior to Batch Start: 100% refund of tuition fees paid, minus any non-refundable payment gateway processing charges, provided written notice is received at least 48 hours before Session 1 commences.
  • After First Weekend Session: If after attending the introductory weekend session you conclude the track is not the right fit, you may request a refund within 48 hours following Session 2. A nominal administrative fee will be deducted.
  • Beyond Week 1: Because live cohorts maintain limited, reserved seat caps, refunds cannot be granted once curriculum progression advances beyond Week 1. In such cases, the student is entitled to the Batch Transfer provision below.

2 Batch Transfer & Rescheduling Flexibility

We recognize that working professionals and university students face unforeseen job deadlines, exams, or personal emergencies.

Enrolled students in good standing may request a one-time batch transfer to a subsequent cohort of the same course track at zero additional tuition cost, subject to seat availability in the incoming batch.

3 Digital Micro-Assets & Downloadable Vaults

All instant-download micro-products (such as practice CSV datasets, viva answer booklets, and cheat sheet bundles) deliver immediate, permanent digital access upon authorization. Consequently, digital downloads are non-refundable once unlocked.

4 Processing Timeline & Mode

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment source (UPI, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Card) via our authorized banking partner.
