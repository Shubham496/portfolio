Terms of Service

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Governs platform usage, course registrations, and educational materials.

1 Institute Purpose & Educational Scope

MYTHOS Online Institute provides live weekend cohort mentorship in Digital Marketing and Data Analytics, alongside self-paced technical documentation and reference curricula in the Resource Library. The platform is designed to impart practical, industry-aligned engineering and marketing competency.

2 Static Reference & Zero Code Execution Platform Rule

All technical examples, SQL scripts, Python routines, and code snippets displayed on the platform are purely educational reference materials. The platform does not provide remote code execution environments, interactive consoles, or cloud sandboxes. Students are expected to configure and maintain their own local development environments (e.g. Python, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Power BI Desktop, VS Code) in accordance with curriculum guidance.

3 Admissions, Cohort Capacity & No Public Pricing

Live cohorts maintain strictly capped batch sizes (typically 20–25 seats) to ensure individualized mentorship. Submitting a registration or enquiry form does not constitute a guaranteed seat until verified by our admissions counseling desk.

In accordance with institute policy, live cohort tuition is never displayed as fixed public prices on browse surfaces; tuition details and scholarship eligibility are discussed transparently during personal counseling and following free demo class participation.

4 Intellectual Property Rights

All original curriculum outlines, syllabus brochures, viva interview banks, and pedagogical articles hosted on MYTHOS are proprietary educational assets. Learners are granted a personal, non-transferable license to read, copy, and practice with the code for personal study and career transition. Redistribution, bulk scraping, or commercial resale of institute assets is prohibited.

5 Code of Conduct in Live Cohorts

Students participating in live weekend sessions, breakout discussions, or institute community channels are expected to maintain professional courtesy, punctuality, and mutual respect. Disruptive, abusive, or unauthorized promotional behavior may result in immediate revocation of batch access.
