Data Privacy & Communication Standards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Effective for all visitors, prospective students, and enrolled cohort members.

Plain-Language Privacy Commitment

At MYTHOS Online Institute, we treat your contact information with strict confidentiality. We only collect the details needed to provide course counseling, syllabus brochures, free demo class access, and batch scheduling. We do not sell, rent, or trade student data to third parties or advertising brokers under any circumstance.

1 Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal information solely to process admissions, deliver course content, and communicate directly with learners:

  • Mandatory Information: Full Name and Mobile / WhatsApp phone number (for course counseling, cohort updates, and batch notifications).
  • Optional Information: Email address (strictly optional across all public student registration and enquiry forms).
  • Academic & Career Context: Current background (e.g. college student, working professional, fresher), prior technical experience, and personal learning goals.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized session IDs, device/browser metadata, and access timestamps to deliver resilient website performance and prevent spam abuse.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use student contact data exclusively for legitimate educational institute operations:

  • Direct counseling by academic advisors regarding weekend cohort curriculum, tools, and batch timings.
  • Transmitting calendar invites, joining links, and reminders for scheduled free demo classes.
  • Delivering syllabus brochures, course guides, and curriculum updates.
  • Student account management and practice dataset downloads within the Resource Library.

3 WhatsApp & Phone Outreach Policy

By submitting your contact number on an enquiry, demo registration, or cohort enrollment form, you grant express consent to be contacted by phone or WhatsApp regarding your educational enquiry.

Right to Opt Out: You have the absolute right to discontinue communications at any time. Simply reply "STOP" or "UNSUBSCRIBE" on WhatsApp, or notify our admissions desk to update your status to do_not_contact, which immediately suppresses all outreach.

4 Security & Access Controls

All student records are stored in secure, access-controlled databases. Administrative access to student phone numbers and application notes is strictly restricted to authenticated admissions personnel via encrypted, signed session tokens. PII in application log streams is masked to prevent unintentional exposure.
