Academic Council & Faculty

Structured Mentorship. Zero Gimmicks.

MYTHOS is a controlled tech learning system. We train working professionals and college seniors on live production workloads through focused weekend cohorts.

One Subject at a Time

We reject shallow surface overviews. Our cohorts focus deeply on one subject per batch (~8 weeks) to ensure true architectural mastery before moving forward.

Discipline Isolation

Our mentors teach exclusively within their specialized domain. Performance Marketing is led by seasoned growth leaders, and Data Analytics by senior data architects.

Production Rigor

Zero simulated toys. Students work directly with real 50k+ row transaction records, live SQL query plans, and real multi-channel ROAS audits.
